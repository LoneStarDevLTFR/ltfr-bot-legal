Lone Tree Fire & Rescue • LTFR Bot

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information LTFR Bot may process or store when used in the Lone Tree Fire & Rescue Discord community.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on which features you use, LTFR Bot may process or store:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, message IDs, role IDs, and usernames/display names.
  • Command usage and administrative action logs.
  • Warnings, strikes, timeouts, suspensions, bans, notes, cases, and moderation history.
  • Shift start/end times, hours, activity records, callsigns, ranks, and certifications.
  • Application responses and application status.
  • Ticket messages, ticket metadata, transcripts, participants, and close reasons.
  • Dispatch call information, incident notes, apparatus assignments, call status, and incident timelines.
  • Suggestion submissions, votes, and suggestion status.
  • Economy balances, transactions, inventory, and related activity.
  • Configuration data needed to operate bot features and permissions.

2. Why We Use This Information

Information is used only to provide and operate LTFR Bot features, including moderation, permissions, department administration, dispatch, applications, tickets, reports, activity tracking, economy features, and server safety.

3. Message Content

The bot may inspect message content when necessary for configured moderation features, protected-user ping detection, ticket transcripts, application workflows, or other bot features that require message text. Message content is not used for advertising.

4. Where Data Is Stored

LTFR Bot currently stores persistent bot data in JSON-based files on the bot’s hosting environment. The bot is hosted using PebbleHost infrastructure. Backups may also be retained by LTFR administrators for reliability and recovery.

5. Who Can Access Data

Access is limited to authorized Lone Tree Fire & Rescue leadership, developers, moderators, or staff members who need access to operate the server and bot. Some data may be visible to other users when a feature is designed to display it publicly, such as suggestions, dispatch calls, leaderboards, or status boards.

6. Third-Party Services

LTFR Bot relies on Discord and its hosting provider to operate. Those services may process technical information under their own privacy policies. LTFR Bot does not sell personal data to advertisers.

7. Data Retention

Data may be retained for as long as it is useful for community operations, moderation history, auditing, safety, recovery, or feature functionality. Some records may be removed or reset by authorized administrators. Backup copies may remain for a limited period after primary data changes.

8. Data Deletion Requests

You may request review or deletion of personal data associated with your Discord account by contacting the LTFR leadership/development team through the official LTFR Discord server or support ticket system. Some records may be retained where necessary for moderation, abuse prevention, security, or administrative recordkeeping.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored bot data, including role-based access controls, restricted administrative commands, hosting access controls, and backups. No system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children and Minors

LTFR Bot is intended to be used only by users who meet Discord’s minimum age requirements in their region. Users should not submit unnecessary sensitive personal information through bot features.

11. No Sale of Personal Data

LTFR Bot does not sell personal data and does not use stored user information for third-party advertising.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as LTFR Bot features, hosting, or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.

13. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests may be directed to the Lone Tree Fire & Rescue leadership/development team through the official LTFR Discord server or support ticket system.
